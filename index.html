Meet your dentist

Care shaped by both Eastern and Western medicine.

Azusa Kaneshima, DDS

Lead Dentist & Practice Director

Dr. Kaneshima studied English at the University of Hawaii, became a nationally licensed acupuncturist, then retrained as a dentist in her 30s while raising a family. In 2019 she founded Iris Dental Care to serve her hometown and the families of Camp Zama.
