Meet Dr. Kaneshima

Professional background & biography

After graduating from high school, Dr. Kaneshima moved to Hawaii to study English at the University of Hawaii, while also taking advantage of the warm climate to rehabilitate a lower back condition. During her time there, she studied English while incorporating swimming and surfing into her rehabilitation.

While living in Hawaii, she met a renowned acupuncturist who inspired her interest in Eastern medicine. After returning to Japan, she enrolled in a specialized acupuncture school and obtained national licenses in acupuncture, moxibustion, and anma massage and shiatsu therapy. She then gained clinical experience as a licensed practitioner of Eastern medicine.

Later, she developed a strong interest in dentistry, a field of Western medicine that requires precision, technical skill, and delicate hands-on techniques. In her 30s, she decided to pursue a new career as a dentist. While balancing her studies with motherhood and raising a family, she successfully graduated from dental school and passed the Japanese National Dental Examination.

Today, Dr. Kaneshima draws upon her background in both Eastern and Western medicine to provide compassionate, personalized dental care tailored to each patient’s individual needs.

Our history

From Iris Dental Care to Iris Dental Medical

Our mission remains the same: to provide compassionate, personalized care while supporting the health and well-being of our diverse community.

  1. 2019

    Iris Dental Care opens

    Dr. Kaneshima founded Iris Dental Care with the desire to serve both the local community where she grew up and the families living and working at Camp Zama. Drawing on her English language skills and international experience, she wanted to create a welcoming dental practice where patients from diverse backgrounds could feel comfortable and receive quality care.

  2. SINCE OPENING

    A community from more than 15 countries

    Our clinic has provided dental care to Japanese patients, members of the U.S. military community and their families, as well as international patients representing more than 15 countries.

  3. OUR SEVENTH YEAR

    Welcoming an American physician

    Through a special program established by the Japanese government, we welcomed an American physician. This marked the beginning of our transition from a dental practice to a comprehensive medical and dental care facility, allowing us to take a more integrated approach to our patients’ overall health and well-being.

  4. TODAY

    Medical and dental, working together

    Our medical and dental teams work closely together, combining their expertise to help patients access high-quality, comprehensive healthcare.

Why choose Iris Dental Medical?

Healthcare that feels familiar, wherever you’re from

At Iris Dental Medical, we are continuously evolving to create a healthcare experience that feels as familiar, comfortable, and convenient as possible for patients from abroad.

Our goal is to provide American-style healthcare in Japan, where patients can feel at home without the stress of language barriers or cultural differences.

An American-style experience

From medical consultations and patient communication to billing and payment procedures, we strive to incorporate the best aspects of the American healthcare experience into our practice.

American medicine, Japanese precision

A unique collaboration between an American physician trained in the United States and a Japanese dentist who brings the precision, attention to detail, and spirit of Japanese hospitality (Omotenashi) to every aspect of patient care.

Care for the whole person

An integrated medical and dental approach that focuses not only on individual symptoms or conditions, but also on each patient’s overall health and well-being.

We believe that exceptional healthcare goes beyond medical treatment. It is about understanding our patients, respecting their backgrounds, and providing compassionate, personalized care they can trust.

Our vision

“The Comfort of American Healthcare, the Precision and Hospitality of Japan.”

Bringing together the best of both worlds to create a truly unique healthcare experience.
