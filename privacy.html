Privacy policy

Effective November 20, 2026

This policy explains what information Iris Dental Medical (アイリス歯科相武台前, “we”, “us”) collects through this website and when you book or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information.

Who we are

Iris Dental Medical

Information we collect

When you visit this website

We use Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. These tools use cookies and similar technologies to collect information such as the pages you view, how long you stay, the device and browser you use, your approximate location (city or region), the website that referred you, and clicks on buttons such as “Book” and our phone number. This information does not tell us your name, and we do not use it to identify you.

When you book an appointment

Online booking is provided by IntakeQ, an appointment and patient-intake service. The information you enter there (for example your name, contact details, insurance information and health history) is collected through IntakeQ on our behalf and is also covered by IntakeQ’s own privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you email or call us, we keep the information you share so we can respond.

How we use information

  • To schedule appointments, provide dental care and keep the records we are required to keep
  • To process insurance claims with your insurance provider when you ask us to
  • To answer your questions and contact you about your appointments
  • To understand which pages and information help patients, and to improve this website

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that help us run the clinic and this website, such as Google (analytics) and IntakeQ (booking)
  • With your insurance provider, where needed to process a claim
  • When required by law

Cookies and analytics choices

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also stop Google Analytics from collecting data about your visits by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on. To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see Google’s explanation.

Links to other websites

This website links to other sites, including our booking site, Torii Health and map services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access. We keep patient records for as long as Japanese law requires, and other information only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your choices

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, or to correct, stop using or delete it, subject to legal requirements. Please email irisdentalcaregate4@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with its effective date at the top.
