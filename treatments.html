General & family care

Everyday dental care for adults and children.

Checkups & cleaning

Routine dental checkups and professional cleaning to keep your teeth and gums healthy.

Children’s dentistry

Gentle checkups and cleaning for children aged 12 and under, so they grow up comfortable at the dentist.

Consultation & examination

Have a concern or some discomfort? We examine your mouth, with on-site CT and digital X-ray when needed, explain what we find, and talk you through your treatment options and fees. Treatment can often begin the same day.

General treatment

Ongoing care to restore and protect your teeth.

  • Cavity treatment
  • Root canal treatment
  • Inlays
  • Crowns
  • Bridges
  • Dentures
  • Night guards

Orthodontics & implants

Planning longer-term treatment, starting with a consultation.

Orthodontics

Straighter teeth and a healthier bite. We assess your alignment and talk through the options, timeline and costs that suit you. Invisalign consultations are free and include a 3D scan, so you can see how your teeth could move before deciding.

  • Wire braces
  • Invisalign
  • Free Invisalign consultation

Dental implants

A long-lasting option for replacing missing teeth. We check whether implants are right for you and explain the options, timeline and costs.

Cosmetic care

Brighter, more confident smiles.

Teeth whitening

Professional whitening for a brighter, more even smile.

Tooth jewelry

A small gem for a little sparkle, applied or removed in a quick visit with no drilling or damage to your teeth.

Cosmetic dentistry

Treatments to refresh your smile and features, planned around what you’d like to achieve.

  • Direct bonding veneers
  • Hyaluronic acid lip & dermal fillers

In our clinic

Technology and privacy that make every visit easier.

  • Free Invisalign consultation

    Includes a 3D scan, so you can see how your alignment could improve.

  • CT & digital X-ray

    On-site imaging for precise diagnosis and treatment planning.

  • 3 fully private rooms

    Every appointment takes place in its own private treatment room.
